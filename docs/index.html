How It Works

Prompt to structured model, then refine

Geomancer keeps the workflow simple: describe the idea, build a deterministic plan, edit the parameters, and export when the model is ready.

1

Describe your idea

Write the part, prop, or hybrid object you want in plain language.

2

Geomancer interprets it

The local AI turns the request into a structured plan with usable parameters.

3

The plan is validated

Geomancer checks the structure and builds the geometry deterministically.

4

Edit and refine

Adjust the parameters in the right rail and regenerate without starting over.

5

Export or continue later

Export STL from the final artifact or reopen a saved model and keep building.

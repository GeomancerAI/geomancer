Workflow

Prompt, edit, regenerate, export

1

Describe the part

Write what you want in plain language, including shape, function, style, and constraints.

2

Geomancer builds a plan

Local AI turns the request into structured parameters that drive deterministic geometry.

3

Edit and regenerate

Adjust dimensions and features, then regenerate from the plan instead of re-prompting from scratch.

4

Open a saved model

Reopen models with plan data, continue editing, and keep iterating over time.

5

Export STL or refine in Blender

Review the final artifact, export STL, or move into Blender for further work.
